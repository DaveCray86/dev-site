What is included:

• 5-page professional website
  You get a complete website with up to 5 pages.  
  Typical pages include:
  - Home
  - About
  - Services (or Products)
  - Contact
  - One extra page of your choice (FAQ, Testimonials, Gallery, etc.)

• Mobile-friendly design
  The website will look good and work properly on phones, tablets, and computers.  
  Most people browse on their phone, so this is essential.

• Contact form
  A form on the website where visitors can send you messages.  
  Messages will go to your email.

• Domain + 1 year hosting
  - Domain = the website address (example: yourbusiness.com)
  - Hosting = the service that keeps your website online 24/7
  
  I will help you register the domain (or use one you already own) and set up hosting for the first year so the site stays live. After the first year, you will pay the renewal fees directly (usually $15–$40/year for the domain + $50–$150/year for hosting depending on the provider).

• Basic SEO setup
  SEO means Search Engine Optimization — making it easier for Google to find and understand your website.
  
  Basic SEO includes:
  - Proper page titles and descriptions
  - Clean website structure
  - Setting up Google Analytics (so you can see how many people visit)
  - Submitting the site to Google Search Console
  - Making sure the site loads reasonably fast
  
  This does NOT include ongoing SEO work, blog writing, or advanced ranking campaigns.

• 1 revision round
  After I show you the first version of the website, you can request changes one time.  
  This covers text changes, image swaps, small layout adjustments, etc.  
  Major redesigns or adding many new pages are not included in the revision round.

• 30 days support
  For 30 days after the website goes live, I will fix any technical issues or small problems that come up at no extra cost.

=====================================
IMPORTANT NOTES FOR CLIENTS
=====================================

• Domain & Hosting Renewals
  After the first year, domain and hosting fees are paid by the client directly to the providers. These are normal ongoing costs of having a website.

• Content Responsibility
  Clients are responsible for providing logos, photos, and key business information. I can help write or improve text, but the facts and brand materials need to come from the client.

• Revisions
  Revision rounds are for refining the work that was already agreed upon. They are not meant for adding major new features or completely changing direction.

• Ownership
  Once the project is paid in full and delivered, the client owns the website or app (including source code for apps).

• Support vs Maintenance
  - Support = fixing problems that shouldn’t be there
  - Maintenance = making updates or small improvements after launch
