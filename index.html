About

Who is behind Creighton Digital?

Creighton Digital is run by David Creighton, a developer based in Santa Maria on California’s Central Coast. He builds professional websites and custom Android apps for businesses that want clear results without agency complexity or inflated pricing.

David takes the technical skills he has built over the years and puts them to work solving real problems for real businesses. Whether it is a clean, professional website that brings in more customers, or a custom Android app that makes someone’s work easier, the goal is simple: deliver something useful that works the way it should.

His background in high-responsibility roles taught him the value of following through and getting the details right. That same approach shows up in every project. He listens, builds carefully, and stays available so clients are never left guessing.

Creighton Digital serves clients worldwide while remaining rooted in the Central Coast of California.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers to the questions most businesses ask before getting started.

How much does a professional website cost?
Creighton Digital website packages start at $799 for a professional 5-page site. The Growth package is $1,499 and the Premium package starts at $2,499. Every package includes mobile-friendly design, SEO setup, domain + 1 year hosting, and support.
What is included in a done-for-you website package?
Every package includes a professional multi-page website, mobile-friendly design, contact form, domain registration plus one year of hosting, SEO setup, revision rounds, and post-launch support. Higher packages add custom design, more pages, advanced forms, and longer support.
Do you build custom Android apps?
Yes. Custom Android apps start at $2,999 for focused utility or content apps. Business apps with backend integration, user accounts, and push notifications start at $5,999. Complex or multi-platform projects are quoted individually.
Do you only work with local businesses?
No. Creighton Digital is based in Santa Maria on California’s Central Coast but works with clients worldwide. All communication is handled remotely by email and screen sharing.
How long does a website project take?
Most Starter and Growth websites are completed within 2–4 weeks after content and feedback are received. Premium or more complex projects typically take 4–8 weeks depending on scope.
What happens after the support period ends?
After the included support window, ongoing maintenance and updates are available for a simple monthly or hourly rate. You are never locked into a long-term contract.
Who is a good fit for Creighton Digital?
Small to mid-sized businesses that want a clean, professional website or Android app without dealing with large agencies, unclear pricing, or long timelines. Ideal clients value clear communication and results.
