About

Who I Am

I’m David Creighton, and I started Creighton Digital to help people get the websites and apps they actually need — without the confusion, delays, or inflated prices that usually come with it.

I take the technical skills I’ve built over the years and put them to work solving real problems for real businesses. Whether it’s a clean, professional website that brings in more customers, or a custom Android app that makes someone’s work easier, my goal is simple: deliver something useful that works the way it should.

My background in high-responsibility roles taught me the value of following through and getting the details right. That same approach shows up in every project I take on. I listen, I build carefully, and I stay available so you’re never left guessing.

If you’re looking for someone who will use their skills to help your business move forward — not just hand you a finished file and disappear — I’d be glad to talk.
