Process & Approach

How does Android app development work with Creighton Digital?

Projects start with a clear conversation about what the app needs to do. From there, the work moves through design, development, testing, and launch preparation. Communication stays direct throughout.

What kinds of apps are a good fit?

  • Utility and productivity tools
  • Business process apps
  • Content or membership apps
  • Specialized tools (for example training or tracking apps)
  • Apps that need clean architecture and reliable performance

Technical approach

Apps are built with modern Android tools and clean architecture. The focus is on reliability, maintainability, and a good user experience rather than unnecessary complexity.

Support after launch

Each package includes a support window. After that, ongoing maintenance, updates, and new features can be handled on a simple monthly or project basis with no long-term lock-in.
