Our Home

Care that feels like family

Harmony House is a licensed Residential Care Facility for the Elderly dedicated to helping seniors live with dignity, comfort, and connection. We believe good care starts with knowing each resident as a person — not just a care plan.

Our small, home-like setting allows us to provide personalized attention while creating a calm, welcoming environment for residents and their families.

Whether your loved one needs assistance with daily activities, medication management, or simply a safe and engaging place to call home, we are here to support them with respect and genuine care.

Services

Support tailored to each resident

We provide personalized assistance that adapts as needs change, always focused on comfort, safety, and quality of life.

Assistance with Daily Living

Help with bathing, dressing, grooming, mobility, and other everyday activities — delivered with patience and dignity.

Medication Management

Careful coordination and administration of medications, with clear communication to families and healthcare providers.

Nutritious Meals

Home-cooked meals prepared with care, accommodating dietary needs and preferences in a shared dining experience.

Activities & Engagement

Daily opportunities for social connection, light exercise, hobbies, and meaningful activities that support well-being.

24/7 Supervision

Awake staff around the clock to provide safety, support, and peace of mind for residents and families.

Family Communication

Regular updates and open communication so families stay informed and involved in their loved one’s care.

FAQ

Common questions

Clear answers for families exploring residential care options.

What is an RCFE?

An RCFE (Residential Care Facility for the Elderly) is a licensed home that provides care and supervision for seniors who need help with daily living but do not require skilled nursing care.

What services does Harmony House provide?

We offer assistance with daily living, medication management, nutritious meals, activities and engagement, 24/7 supervision, and regular communication with families.
