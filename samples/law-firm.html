About

Steady guidance you can trust

Crestwood Law was built on a simple idea: clients deserve clear explanations, honest assessments, and attorneys who follow through.

We work primarily with individuals and small businesses on the Central Coast. Whether you need a contract reviewed, an estate plan put in place, or counsel on a business decision, the goal is the same — practical help that moves you forward.

No unnecessary jargon. No pressure. Just careful work and direct communication.
