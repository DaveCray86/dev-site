About

Straightforward rentals, well-kept homes

Coastal Rentals manages a small collection of quality homes and units on California’s Central Coast. We focus on clean, well-maintained properties and clear communication with tenants.

Whether you’re looking for a quiet cottage, a family home, or a simple one-bedroom, our goal is to make the process easy and transparent from the first inquiry through move-in.

Process

How to apply

A simple path from inquiry to keys.

  • 1

    Browse & inquire

    View the photo galleries and send a message about the home you’re interested in.

  • 2

    Schedule a showing

    We’ll arrange a time for you to see the property in person.

  • 3

    Submit an application

    Complete a straightforward application with standard screening.

  • 4

    Move in

    Once approved, sign the lease and schedule your move-in date.

What we typically look for

  • Stable income (generally 2.5–3× rent)
  • Positive rental history
  • Background & credit screening
  • Clear communication throughout the process
